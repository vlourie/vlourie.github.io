XP Mirror — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

What the app does

XP Mirror is a command-line tool used only by its author. It signs in to the author's own Google account with the drive.file scope and creates files (public release archives of the X-Piratez HD mod) in a folder in the author's Google Drive. It can see only files that it created itself.

Data we collect

None about other people. The app has no users other than its author, has no server, and does not collect, sell, share or transmit personal data to anyone. The OAuth refresh token is stored only on the author's own computer.

Data users of the mod download

People who update the mod with its launcher download public files (game mod archives) from a public Google Drive folder using the Google Drive API. Google may process the network request under its own privacy policy; XP Mirror does not receive or store any information about those downloads.

Google user data

XP Mirror's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
